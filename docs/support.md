# Support

Need help or want to contribute? Here are the ways you can get support and engage with the λLoop community.

## Report Bugs

If you encounter any issues or bugs, please report them on our [GitHub Issues tracker](https://github.com/stefanofornari/lambda-loop/issues).

## Discussions

Have a question or an idea? Feel free to open a discussion in the [GitHub Discussions section](https://github.com/stefanofornari/lambda-loop/discussions).

## Vote in the Poll

We value your feedback! Please take a moment to vote in our poll: [Poll](https://github.com/stefanofornari/lambda-loop/discussions/2)

## Chat with Us

You can also get in touch with the developers and other users on our Zulip chat:
[Join the conversation on Zulip](https://lloop.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/548151-general)
